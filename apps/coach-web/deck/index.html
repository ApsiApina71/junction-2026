The problem

Your bank knows every euro you spend. Ask where it went, and you get a list.

Statements and transaction lists are records, not answers. They do not say what is safe to spend this week, what kind of spender you are, or what one small change would add up to.

  • What can I safely spend this week?
  • Where did my money really go?
  • How much would I save if I ate out 20% less?
Kolme koria2
The idea

Three baskets. That is the whole lesson.

Kolme koria is Finnish for three baskets. Everyone’s spending falls into them, and seeing your own split is where understanding starts.

Regular essentials

Mandatory and regular. Rent, bills, groceries, the commute.

Unexpected necessities

Mandatory but irregular. The dentist, a repair, the washing machine that gave up.

Everyday spending

What slips through your fingers. Coffees, eating out, taxis, small treats.

Your data, your labels. Move anything to the basket where it belongs for you, and every figure follows.

The product

One number for the week. And the sum behind it.

Safe to spend has two limits: what the balance allows and what this pay cycle allows. The lower one wins, and every line is there to check.

Talk with your money

Where it goes in three baskets. A what-if slider that becomes a four-week challenge. Spender types from measurable habits. In Finnish and English.
