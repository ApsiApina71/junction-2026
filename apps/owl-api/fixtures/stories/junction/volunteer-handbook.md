# Junction 2026: volunteer handbook

The handbook for the Junction volunteer crew. In customer service mode at the info desk, Owl suggests answers only from this handbook (JUN-105). Made up for the demo video. It is not Junction's official handbook.

## Final deadline

The final deadline is Sunday at 10:00.
By the deadline, every team sets its project to FINAL on Junction Platform.
Peer review of the final projects starts at 10:30 and ends at 13:00.

## Recycling round

Cans and bottles with the deposit mark go in the round CANS opening of the white sorting bin, not the plastic.
Plastic packaging without a deposit mark goes in the black square PLASTIC opening.
Empty cups go to the cardboard bin. Food waste goes to the bio bin.
When a bin is full, tie the bag and put a new one in. Full bags go to the waste room next to the loading dock.

## Info desk

The info desk is open all night. Two volunteers are at the desk at all times.
Hackers get their wristband at the info desk. Check the name against the participant list.

## Safety

In an emergency, call 112 first and then the shift manager.
First aid kits are at the info desk and in the main hall.
Keep the emergency exits clear at all times.
