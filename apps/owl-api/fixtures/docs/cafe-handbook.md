# Café Kide: counter handbook

Example knowledge base for the Owl demo. In customer service mode, Owl suggests answers only from documents like this one.

## Opening hours

- Monday to Friday 7:30–18:00.
- Saturday 9:00–16:00.
- Sunday closed.
- On public holidays we open at 10:00 and close at 15:00.

## Prices

- Filter coffee 3.20 €, refill 1 € with the same cup.
- Cappuccino and latte 4.50 €. Oat milk or soy milk costs nothing extra.
- Cinnamon bun 3.80 €. Rye sandwich 7.90 €.
- Takeaway cup: bring your own cup and get 0.30 € off.

## Returns and refunds

- A drink that is wrong or cold is remade at once, free of charge. No receipt needed.
- Unopened coffee beans can be returned within 14 days with the receipt.
- Refunds go back to the card that paid. Cash is refunded in cash.
- Opened coffee beans cannot be returned. Offer a different roast instead.

## Allergens

- The allergen list for every product is in the blue folder next to the till.
- The cinnamon buns contain gluten, milk and egg. The rye sandwich contains gluten.
- We have gluten-free oat cookies, but they are made in a kitchen that also handles wheat.
- If you are not sure, never guess: show the customer the allergen folder or ask the shift lead.

## Loyalty card

- Every coffee gets one stamp. The tenth coffee is free.
- A lost card cannot be replaced, but you can start a new one.

## Payment

- We take cards, phone payments and cash. The smallest card payment is 1 €.
- Lunch vouchers (Edenred, ePassi) are accepted for food, not for packaged products.

## Wi-Fi and seats

- The Wi-Fi network is KideGuest. The password is on the receipt.
- Seats by the window are free for everyone; the big table can be booked for groups of six or more.
