# Kide Logistics: warehouse handbook

Example company handbook for the Owl demo. Owl's Ask answers only from documents like this one.

## Your shift

- The shift starts at 7:00. Sign in at the office terminal and pick up your scanner.
- Safety shoes and the reflective vest are always on in the warehouse.
- Breaks: 10 minutes at 9:00, lunch 30 minutes at 11:30, 10 minutes at 14:00.
- Your shift lead is Laura. If you cannot find her, call the office: extension 100.

## Picking an order

- Take one order sheet at a time from the "Next" tray.
- Use a medium box (M) for up to five items. Use a large box (L) only for bulky items.
- Pick the items in shelf order: aisle A first, then B, then C.
- Always check the size and amount on the label. The 0.5 l and 1 l oat drink cartons look almost the same.
- Check every order against the order sheet before you pack it.

## Out of stock

If an item is out of stock, mark the line as missing on the order and tell the shift lead. Never swap in another product without asking the shift lead.

## Damaged items

A dented can or a leaking carton never goes to a customer. Put it in the red "Damaged" box at the end of aisle C and pick a new one.

## Packing

- Pack heavy items at the bottom and fill empty space with paper so nothing moves.
- Glass and eggs go in a bubble wrap bag first.
- Tape the box shut with two strips of tape.
- The label always goes on the top side, never over the tape.
- Put finished boxes on the outgoing pallet by the door. The truck leaves at 15:00.

## Cold products

Chilled items (dairy, oat drink from the cold room) are picked last and packed within 15 minutes. Use a cold bag for them if the order has more than two chilled items.

## Safety

- Never climb the shelves. Use the step ladder for the top shelf.
- Lift with your legs, not your back. Ask for help with anything over 20 kg.
- If you see a spill, put the yellow sign next to it and tell the shift lead.
- In a fire alarm, leave everything and go to the meeting point in the car park.
