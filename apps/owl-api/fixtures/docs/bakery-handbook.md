# Leipomo Ruis: bakery handbook

Example company handbook for the Owl demo (JUN-74). Owl's Ask answers only from documents like this one.

## Your shift

- The morning shift starts at 4:30. The first rye bread must be out of the oven by 7:00, when the shop opens.
- Wash your hands and put on a hairnet, a clean apron and gloves before you touch any dough.
- Your shift lead is Helena. If you cannot find her, call the shop: extension 20.

## Hygiene

- Wash your hands for 30 seconds: when you arrive, after every break, after the toilet and after touching raw eggs or the bins.
- Jewellery and watches stay in your locker.
- A plaster on your hand must be blue, and you wear a glove over it.
- If you have a stomach illness, do not come to work. Call Helena.

## Allergens

- Our rye bread contains rye (gluten). It contains no milk, eggs or nuts.
- Seeded loaves contain sesame. Sesame is never used on the rye line before 7:00.
- Clean the work table and the scale between rye bread and anything with sesame or nuts.
- If a customer asks about allergens, show the allergen list by the till. Never guess.

## The dough mixer

- The mixer has a lever with three positions: 0 = stop, 1 = slow, 2 = fast.
- Rye dough is always mixed on 1 (slow) for 8 minutes. Rye dough on 2 overheats and goes sticky.
- The safety guard must be closed before the lever leaves 0. If the guard opens, the mixer stops; put the lever back to 0 before you close it.
- Never put a hand or a scraper in the bowl while the lever is on 1 or 2.

## Weighing

- Weigh flour on the big floor scale. Press TARE with the empty bowl on the scale first.
- The morning rye batch: 2.0 kg rye flour, 1.2 kg sourdough, 1.4 l water at 35 °C and 40 g salt.
- The sourdough bucket must have today's date on its label. Old sourdough goes back to Helena.

## Shaping

- Cut the dough into eight pieces of 600 g. Check the weight of every second piece.
- Shape round loaves and put them seam side down on the tray, four in a row, two rows.
- Let the loaves rise under a cloth for 45 minutes.

## The ovens

- Oven 1 is for buns and pastries. Oven 2 is for rye bread only.
- Rye bread bakes in oven 2 at 230 °C for 50 minutes. Press the steam button once when the trays go in.
- Always use the long oven gloves. The oven door and the trays are over 200 °C.
- Set the timer every time. Never trust your memory with the ovens.
- The bread is done when it sounds hollow when you knock on the bottom.

## Cooling and the shop

- Cool the loaves on the rack for at least 60 minutes before they are sliced or bagged.
- Label every bag with the baking date.
- Bread that is two days old goes to the "Yesterday's bread" shelf at half price.

## Safety

- A burn: cool it under cold running water for 20 minutes, then tell Helena. The first aid kit is next to the sink.
- Spilled flour makes the floor slippery. Sweep it at once.
- The fire blanket is by oven 2. In a fire, press the red emergency stop by the door.
